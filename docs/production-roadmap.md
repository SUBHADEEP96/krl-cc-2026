# Production roadmap

## Already production-compatible
Strict domain types, pure scoring, central permissions and public serializer; stable UUID/passport split; relational PostGIS migration; evidence classification; responsive accessible UI; deterministic test fixtures.

## Prototype-only
Local authentication/storage, simulated GPS/uploads, stylised map, client-side aggregation, partial Bengali and compact operational forms.

## Before a real pilot
Complete field research and consent language; Supabase adapter/Auth/MFA; server-side permission and score enforcement; storage antivirus/metadata stripping; complete translations; device testing; accessibility audit; monitoring and alerting; PITR backups and restore drills; DPIA/security review; training and support runbooks; legacy-data mapping and rehearsal.

## Before 5,000-farm rollout
PostGIS tile/cluster service, materialised aggregates, queues and background jobs, offline-first IndexedDB sync with conflict resolution, image derivatives/CDN, load and disaster-recovery tests, SLOs, audit-log retention, key rotation, penetration test, district training programme, tiered support and change governance.
