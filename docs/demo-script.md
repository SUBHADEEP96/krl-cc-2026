2. **0:35** On `/dashboard`, frame statewide KPIs, score trajectory, attention queue and funding.
3. **1:30** Open `/map`; filter **Nadia Warriors** and **Nadia**, noting URL-synchronised filters and accessible results.

6. **4:45** Return to Overview and point out the new timeline activity and recalculated current score; refresh to demonstrate persistence.
7. **5:30** Switch to Judge, open `/judging`, review audit/evidence summary, confirm conflict status and approve the score.
8. **6:30** Open `/leaderboard`; show the farm’s approved score and deterministic ranking.
9. **7:15** Open the Story tab and public passport. Confirm it shows district-level location and approved story but no identity details, coordinates, private finances, issues or judge notes.
10. **7:50** Close with the Supabase/PostGIS production path and reset control.
